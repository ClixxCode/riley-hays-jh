Why call Riley Hays?

Customer-first roofing — every single job.

Our experienced and trained staff at Riley Hays Roofing & Construction have always put the customer first. Some roofing contractors install a roof and call it a day, but we're different.

Installing or repairing a roof is an investment, and you have preferences that deserve to be met. We'll come out to your home or business and give you a free estimate on your project.

We pride ourselves on tailoring our services to your needs and getting the job done right the first time. Our team trains continually on the latest roofing materials, methods, and techniques, and we back every job with exceptional warranties.

Repairs, gutters & storm damage

Arkansas roofing repairs, gutters & storm damage.

Leaky roof? Hail, wind, or rain damage? No problem.

Living in Arkansas, we all know weather can take a hefty toll on your home or business's roof. Whether the damage is from wind, hail, or rain, the experienced contractors at Riley Hays have the tools and training to put things right.

When you discover a leak or storm damage, replacing the entire roof isn't always necessary. While a full replacement might be more profitable for us, our crew is more interested in taking care of what you actually need.
